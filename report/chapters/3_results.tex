\section{Validation Strategy and Metrics}
\label{sec:validation}

We evaluate each feature/model configuration with the same repeated stratified
five-fold splits (three repeats, seed~$42$ for reproducibility). Imputation,
one-hot encoding, and scaling are fitted inside each training fold. We report
fold means for training and validation accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and
ROC-AUC, plus the population standard deviation of validation scores. OOF
probabilities are averaged across repeats and thresholded at~$0.5$. The ensemble
and its individual learners are scored on the same folds.
